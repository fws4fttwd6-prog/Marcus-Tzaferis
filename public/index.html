What will it actually cost me?

Enter a price from anywhere in the world and see what lands in Toronto, line by line.

Read this before you spend money. Landed costs are estimates built from published duty, excise and LCBO rates that change without notice; the LCBO markup is the softest number in the calculation. Ontario requires alcohol imported for personal use to be consigned through the LCBO. Prices and stock come from merchant pages at the moment of searching and go stale quickly. Verify with the merchant and with LCBO Private Ordering before committing to an order.

Vintage scores are an editorial consensus of the major published charts, kept in src/data/vintage-chart.ts. Producer matters more than vintage — a fine grower in an 88-point year beats a lazy one in a 97-point year.
